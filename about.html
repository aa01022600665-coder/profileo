About Us

The Story Behind
Profileo

We're building the most powerful anti-detect browser for professionals who need to manage multiple online identities safely and efficiently.

Our Mission

Profileo was born from a simple observation: managing multiple online accounts shouldn't be complicated or risky. Whether you're a digital marketer, e-commerce seller, social media manager, or affiliate professional, you need separate browser identities that don't get linked.

We set out to build a tool that makes multi-account management accessible to everyone — not just tech experts. With unique browser fingerprints, built-in proxy management, and powerful automation, Profileo gives you complete control over your online presence.

Our team is dedicated to privacy, security, and simplicity. We believe that everyone deserves the freedom to manage their digital identity on their own terms.
