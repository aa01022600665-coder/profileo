General

Profileo is a desktop application that lets you create and manage multiple browser profiles, each with a unique digital fingerprint. It's designed for professionals who need to manage multiple online accounts safely.

Each browser profile gets a unique set of browser parameters — Canvas fingerprint, WebGL renderer, Audio context, screen resolution, timezone, language, and more. Websites see each profile as a completely different device and user.

Profileo is currently available for Windows 10 and Windows 11. macOS and Linux versions are planned for future releases.

Profileo supports Chrome, Brave, Edge, Opera, and Yandex. You can choose a different browser for each profile while maintaining consistent fingerprint emulation.

Yes, you can log in to your Profileo account on different computers. Your profiles and settings are synced to your account. However, only one session can be active at a time per account.

Browser Profiles

The number of profiles depends on your plan: Mini (10), Starter (50), Professional (150), Expert (350), and Business (1000). You can upgrade anytime to get more profiles.

Profileo spoofs Canvas, WebGL, Audio context, WebRTC, screen resolution, timezone, language, user agent, fonts, hardware concurrency, device memory, platform, and many more parameters for each profile.

Yes, you can export profiles as backup files and import them on another computer. This is useful for transferring your setup between devices or sharing profiles with team members.

Your profile configurations are synced to your Profileo account in the cloud. If you uninstall and reinstall the app, you can log back in and your profiles will be restored. Local browser data (cookies, cache) may need to be reconfigured.

Proxies

Yes! Profileo has a built-in proxy manager that supports HTTP, SOCKS4, and SOCKS5 proxies. You can assign different proxies to each profile and manage them from a central dashboard.

While not strictly required, using unique proxies for each profile is strongly recommended. Without proxies, all your profiles will share the same IP address, which can lead to account linking.

Profileo does not sell proxies directly. You can use any third-party proxy provider. We recommend residential proxies for the best results and have guides on how to configure them.

Automation

Yes, Profileo supports custom Puppeteer scripts. You can write your own automation logic or modify existing scripts. We also provide a visual script builder for non-technical users.
